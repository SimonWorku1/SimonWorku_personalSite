About

Hi, my name is Simon Worku.

I'm a senior undergrad at LMU studying Computer Science with a minor in Computer Engineering. I really like building things from top to bottom, starting with the first rough idea and staying with it all the way down to the last line of code. I like owning what I make and knowing how every part of it works. So far that's meant a real-time multiplayer game, a social platform, and an AI evaluation suite, and with each one I try to sweat the small stuff and make something people genuinely enjoy using.
